FAQ

Common Questions

How does your placement fee work?

Our fee is a percentage of the candidate’s first-year base salary, ranging from 15% to 22% depending on role function and seniority (three tiers: Entry & Support 15%, Professional & Skilled 18%, Leadership & Specialized 22%). It is contingency-based — you pay only when we successfully place a candidate. There are no upfront costs and no retainer required. Current rates are introductory Founder’s Pricing, locked through 2026 for early clients.

What is your replacement guarantee?

If a placed candidate leaves or is let go within 90 days of their start date, we will conduct one full replacement search at no additional charge. The guarantee applies to all placements at every tier.

Do you require an exclusivity agreement?

No. We don’t require exclusivity. That said, clients who work with us on a focused basis typically see faster results — the more context we have about your role, culture, and timeline, the tighter the shortlist.

What do you need from us to start a search?

A kickoff call, a job description (or the information to build one), your target salary range, and a point of contact for scheduling. We handle the rest. Most clients are receiving a candidate shortlist within two weeks of kickoff.

How do you screen candidates?

Every candidate on a shortlist has cleared resume screening, a structured phone or video interview, and a reference or background check as appropriate. We don’t forward résumés we haven’t personally reviewed — your shortlist contains qualified finalists, not volume.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. In fact, small and mid-size businesses are our primary focus. We understand that you need professional HR support without the overhead of a full internal department. Our model is designed specifically for organizations in that position.

Do you work outside of Columbus, Ohio?

Yes. While our primary market is Greater Columbus and Central Ohio, we conduct searches and provide HR consulting services for clients nationwide. Remote roles are no problem — we source across the country.

How long does a typical search take?

Most searches are completed within 3–6 weeks depending on the role, market conditions, and client responsiveness during the interview process. We keep you updated throughout so there are no surprises.

Do you charge candidates?

No. Candidates are never charged — our fee is paid entirely by the hiring employer, so our only obligation to you is an honest process and a role that actually fits. If you are looking for your next role, see our Open Positions.
