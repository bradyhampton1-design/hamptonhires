FAQ

Common Questions

How does your placement fee work?

Our fee is a percentage of the candidate’s first-year base salary, ranging from 13.5% to 18.5% depending on role complexity and seniority. It is contingency-based — you pay only when we successfully place a candidate. There are no upfront costs and no retainer required.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. In fact, small and mid-size businesses are our primary focus. We understand that you need professional HR support without the overhead of a full internal department. Our model is designed specifically for organizations in that position.

How long does a typical search take?

Most searches are completed within 3–6 weeks depending on the role, market conditions, and client responsiveness during the interview process. We keep you updated throughout so there are no surprises.

Do you help candidates directly?

Yes. We offer resume writing, interview coaching, and career document services for job seekers. These services are priced separately from employer engagements. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.
